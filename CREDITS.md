# Asset credits

Third-party assets added to Hu Taong. Keep this file with the game if you share it: the CC BY
licences below require the credit to travel with the work.

The rest of `Resources/` (bg1-bg5, bg9-bg12, the original music and sound effects) was already in the project
and is not covered here.

All butterflies, talismans, lanterns, panels and other UI art are drawn by the game's own code
(`render/art.py`), so there is nothing to credit for them.

are **not** openly licensed: copyright stays with their artists and HoYoverse. They are here for
personal, non-commercial use, so get permission before sharing the game publicly with them included.

All were cropped to 16:9 and scaled to 1920x1080. Those marked "Dimmed" were also darkened by 22% so
they are comfortable to play over.
